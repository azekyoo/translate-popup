# Privacy Policy: Translate Popup

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

Translate Popup is a browser extension that translates text you select on a web page.

## What data is handled

- **Text you select.** When you select text, it is sent to Google Translate
  (`translate.googleapis.com`) to get the translation. Nothing else from the page is sent.
- **Your settings.** Target language, theme and glass effect are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`,
  so they follow your Chrome profile. They never leave Chrome's own sync.

## What is not done

- No analytics, tracking, ads or telemetry.
- No account, no server of our own: the extension talks only to Google Translate.
- Selected text is not stored, logged, sold or shared with anyone other than Google Translate.

## Google

Translation requests go directly from your browser to Google and are covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If Google temporarily blocks your network,
the extension can open Google's verification page; passing it stores a Google cookie in your browser.
